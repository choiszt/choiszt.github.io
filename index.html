Shuai Liu

Ph.D. Student @ MMLab, NTU

I am a Ph.D. student at MMLab@NTU, supervised by Prof. Ziwei Liu.

Prior to this, I was a Founding Team Member at Synvo AI, advised by Prof. Chen Change Loy. I obtained my MEng (by Research) degree at MMLab@NTU, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Ziwei Liu and to have received kind and valuable guidance from Jingkang Yang and Bo Li. I obtained my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from BUPT in 2024.

Additionally, I had a wonderful time as an intern at the Shanghai AI Laboratory under the mentorship of Prof. Ziwei Liu.

My research lies in Multimodality, Egocentric AI, and Agent.

Feel free to reach out for collaborations, questions, or just to chat!
